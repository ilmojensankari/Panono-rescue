# Privacy

Panono Rescue does not require an app account and contains no advertising, tracking, developer telemetry, or automatic cloud upload.

## Camera and files

The app communicates with your Panono camera over its local Wi-Fi connection. Previews are decoded on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad. Original UPF files are saved to your chosen folder on Mac, or to Panono Rescue’s local Files folder on iPhone and iPad. Preview data may remain in the app’s local cache.

Files stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them. On iPhone and iPad, the system share sheet handles that choice. A destination such as iCloud Drive or another app has its own privacy practices.

## Time and location

Camera time is synchronized from your device on connection and before captures. Location tagging is optional and starts off each app session. If you choose device location, macOS or iOS controls permission and supplies the location fix. Manual coordinates are also available.

When tagging is enabled, coordinates are sent to your connected camera and written into the new photo’s metadata. They are not sent to the app developer. Sharing an original that contains location metadata also shares those coordinates. The app does not rewrite metadata in existing camera files.

## Optional support

Donation QR codes are generated locally from fixed public recipient addresses. Copy address uses the system clipboard. Opening Ko-fi launches its website in your browser. Ko-fi and any wallet you choose have their own privacy practices. Panono Rescue does not process or record donations.

## TestFlight beta

When you use a TestFlight build, Apple automatically collects crash reports and usage information and shares them with the app developer. Feedback you submit, including screenshots and comments, is also shared. If you join only through a public invitation link, your name and email address are not shown to the developer. See [Apple’s TestFlight privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/) for the details. This beta reporting is separate from the app’s local camera and file handling.
