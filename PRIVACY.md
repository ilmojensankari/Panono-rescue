# Privacy

Panono Rescue does not require an account and contains no analytics, advertising,
tracking, developer telemetry or automatic cloud upload.

The app communicates with your Panono camera over its local Wi-Fi connection.
Panorama previews are decoded locally on your Mac. Original UPF files are saved
to the folder you select; preview data may remain in the app's temporary storage.

Camera time is synchronized from the Mac on connection and before app captures.
Location tagging is optional and starts Off each app session. If you choose Mac
location, macOS controls permission and supplies the location fix. If tagging is
enabled, the coordinates are sent to your connected camera for the new photo's
metadata. They are not sent to the app developer. Manual coordinates are also
available. The application does not rewrite metadata in existing camera files.

Wallet donation codes are generated locally from fixed public recipient
addresses. Copy address uses the Mac clipboard. Opening Ko-fi launches its
website in your browser; Ko-fi and any wallet you choose have their own privacy
practices. The app does not process or record donations.
